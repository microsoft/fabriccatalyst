### Key Benefits
- 🎯 Weekly Engineering Connection Calls featuring latest product updates
- 📚 Access to sales readiness and enablement resources
- 🎓 Information about trainings, workshops, and certifications
- 💡 Opportunity to provide feedback and influence product development
